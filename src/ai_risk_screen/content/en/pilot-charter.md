# Pilot charter template

Use this charter when the risk screen returns **amber**. Complete all ten fields before starting a controlled pilot.

| # | Field | Guidance |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | **Owner** | Name, role, and contact for the pilot lead |
| 2 | **Task and baseline** | What the pilot does; how the same work is done today without AI |
| 3 | **Permitted inputs** | Data types and sources allowed; explicit exclusions |
| 4 | **Tool and vendor** | Approved tool, version, processing location, contract reference |
| 5 | **Affected-person and partner role** | Who is affected; which partners were consulted and what they said |
| 6 | **Success and subgroup checks** | Success criteria; how you will test for bias or exclusion in marginalised groups |
| 7 | **Human approval** | Who approves outputs before they reach affected people or the public |
| 8 | **Monitoring** | Metrics, review cadence, incident log location |
| 9 | **Stop and remedy** | Failure threshold; withdrawal procedure; remedy path for harm |
| 10 | **Close-out lessons** | What worked, what failed, recommendation to scale, pause, or stop |
